# Services and destinations

Always call get_country_profile for every country in the brief. Use only facts from the
profile or the brief. If something is missing, leave it out; never fill it from memory.

## Services

### Tour package
Sell the experience: 3-5 highlights from the profile, then what is included (only from the
brief), then price and CTA. Angles: dream destination, family memories, value.

### Visit visa assistance
Sell the easy, trusted process, not the visa itself. Structure: problem ("process mushkil
lagta hai?") -> our help (file preparation, appointment booking, document checklist) ->
CTA. Always "visa assistance" or "visa processing help". Never promise approval, timing
or fees unless the profile states them. Good for: Schengen, USA, UK-style long forms,
Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Korea.

### Air ticketing
Route + fare (only if in brief) + "best fares, quick booking on WhatsApp". Visuals: generic
aircraft, airport, destination skyline. No airline logos unless the brief says allowed.

### Umrah package
Respectful and calm. Highlight: package level (economy/standard/premium), nights in Makkah
and Madinah, hotel distance to Haram, transport, visa, ziyarat (only if in brief).
No music words, no jokes, no "masti". Use the Umrah template.

### Hotel booking
Comfort, location, price. One hotel type per post.

### Honeymoon
Romantic but modest: couples walking, sunsets, resorts. No swimwear close-ups or kissing.
Luxury tone.

## Regions and what works
- Gulf and Middle East (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq/Iran ziyarat): Umrah,
  Dubai family trips, desert safari, shopping festivals, ziyarat groups.
- Turkey, Caucasus, Central Asia (Turkey, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan,
  Kyrgyzstan): history + nature, "hidden gem" angle, budget-friendly Europe feel, snow in winter.
- South-East Asia (Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, Bali, Vietnam, Philippines): family and
  honeymoon, beaches, theme parks, halal food (Malaysia, Indonesia).
- East Asia (China, Japan, Korea, Hong Kong): cherry blossom, cities, shopping, visa help.
- South Asia islands (Sri Lanka, Maldives, Nepal): honeymoon, nature, short trips.
- Schengen Europe (29 countries): mostly visa assistance and multi-country tours
  (Paris + Switzerland + Italy). Summer season peak. Use "Schengen visa" as one service
  or name the countries of the tour.
- USA: visit visa assistance, family visits, New York, Orlando, Niagara (US side).
- Australia and New Zealand: visit visa assistance, family visits, Sydney, Melbourne,
  Queenstown, Auckland, nature.

## Multi-country briefs
One highlight block per country, in travel order, one line linking them ("phir chalein Baku").
