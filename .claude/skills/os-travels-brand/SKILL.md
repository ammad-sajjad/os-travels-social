# OS Travels and Tours - brand voice

## Who we are
OS Travels and Tours is a Pakistani travel agency: tour packages, visit visa assistance,
air ticketing, Umrah packages, hotel booking and honeymoon trips across Asia, the Gulf,
Schengen Europe, the USA, Australia and New Zealand. Our audience is Pakistani families,
couples, young professionals and overseas Pakistanis, on mobile, reading Roman Urdu.

## Personality
- Trustworthy first: clear prices, no hidden surprises, honest promises.
- Warm and family-friendly: like an experienced elder brother or sister, not a salesman.
- Excited about travel, never exaggerated.
- Pakistani: everyday spoken Urdu with natural English travel words.

## Tone presets (the brief picks one)
- Exciting: tours and deals. Punchy hooks, "zabardast", "shaandaar".
- Family: family packages, school holidays. Warm, safe, "apni family ke saath".
- Luxury: premium and honeymoon. Calm, elegant, few emojis, "yaadgaar lamhay".
- Budget: low prices, students. Direct, value first, "kam budget mein".
- Religious: Umrah. Respectful, peaceful, no hype, no jokes, no music words.

## Always
- Brand name exactly: OS Travels and Tours (hashtag #OSTravels).
- End with a clear call to action. Default: "Abhi WhatsApp karein" + {{WHATSAPP}}.
  Others: "Seats limited hain, aaj hi book karein", "Details ke liye WhatsApp karein".
- Contact details only from placeholders, never invented:
  {{PHONE}} {{WHATSAPP}} {{WEBSITE}} {{ADDRESS}} {{LICENCE_NO}}

## Good words
safar, khoobsurat, zabardast, shaandaar, yaadgaar, aaraam se, bharosa, family ke saath,
complete package, all inclusive, visa assistance, guided tour, best price.

## Never
- "100% visa guarantee", "visa pakka", "guaranteed approval". We give visa assistance only.
- "Cheapest in Pakistan", "No. 1 agency" or any claim we cannot prove.
- Prices, dates, seats, inclusions, discounts, hotel stars or airlines not in the brief.
- Visa requirements, fees or processing times not in the country profile.
- Fake reviews, testimonials or customer names.
- Hindi-only words: yatra, sundar, dhanyavaad, pariwar (use safar, khoobsurat, shukriya, ghar walay).
- Mocking any country, culture, religion or competitor.

## Numbers
- Price on screen and in captions: PKR 2,45,000/- (Pakistani grouping).
- Dates: "15 March", "15 se 22 March tak". Duration: "7 din 6 raatein".

## Emojis
Instagram 2-5, Facebook 1-3 (e.g. plane, globe, island, phone). Umrah: kaaba and dua hands only, sparingly.
